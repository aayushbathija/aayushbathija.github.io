I’m a high school student in California working on problems in algebraic combinatorics, and I teach mathematics to younger students most weekends.

Research

With Professor Soskin I’ve been extending results on Lorentzian polynomials to the degree-3 case, working on a question about transversal cones. Lorentzian polynomials sit between combinatorics and convex geometry: log-concavity statements that are hard to prove by counting arguments fall out of them fairly cleanly.
